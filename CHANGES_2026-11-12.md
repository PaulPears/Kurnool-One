# Kurnool One – Changelog (November 12, 2026)

Comprehensive summary of all fixes, feature enhancements, security updates, and builds completed across the **Mobile App (`frontend/`)**, **Website & Admin Panel (`website/`)**, and **Firebase (`firestore.rules`)**.

---

## 1. Mobile App UI/UX & Navigation Fixes (`frontend/`)

- **Edge-to-Edge Status Bar Overlap Fixed (All 20+ Screens):**
  - Migrated `SafeAreaView` imports from `react-native` to `react-native-safe-area-context` across all screens (`HomeScreen.tsx`, `DirectoryScreen.tsx`, `ProfileScreen.tsx`, `OffersScreen.tsx`, `ExploreScreen.tsx`, `NewsScreen.tsx`, `ManageBusinessScreen.tsx`, `ManageProfessionalScreen.tsx`, `PartnerProgramScreen.tsx`, `BusinessDetailScreen.tsx`, `ProfessionalDetailScreen.tsx`, `SearchScreen.tsx`, `SettingsScreen.tsx`, `AdminDashboardScreen.tsx`, `NotificationScreen.tsx`, `EditProfileScreen.tsx`, `DisclaimerScreen.tsx`, `PrivacyPolicyScreen.tsx`, `TermsScreen.tsx`).
  - Added `flexShrink: 1` and `numberOfLines={1}` to the `"Kurnool One"` header title in `HomeScreen.tsx` so it never overlaps header buttons on smaller screens.
- **Android Hardware Back Navigation & Tab Bar Insets (`AppNavigator.tsx`):**
  - Added `backBehavior="history"` to `<Tab.Navigator>` so pressing back returns to the exact previous tab/screen.
  - Added `useSafeAreaInsets()` dynamic bottom padding to `tabBarStyle` for Android 15/16 (Target SDK 36) edge-to-edge compatibility.
- **Businesses & Professionals Card Overflow Fixed (`DirectoryScreen.tsx`):**
  - Added `minWidth: 0`, `flexShrink: 1`, and `flexWrap: 'wrap'` to card titles, category pills, service area text, and action button rows so text and buttons stay inside cards on all screen widths.
- **Profile `"ACTIVE"` / `"EARN"` Badge Alignment Fixed (`ProfileScreen.tsx`):**
  - Added `minWidth: 0`, `flexShrink: 1`, and `flexWrap: 'wrap'` to `menuContent` and badge header rows so badges no longer overflow outside cards.
- **Pre-Filled Form Text Fixed:**
  - Cleared pre-filled default strings in `HouseRentScreen.tsx` (`waterFacility`, `parking`) and `RegisterProfessionalScreen.tsx` (`experienceYears`, `serviceAreas`, `visitingCharges`) so inputs start empty and show proper placeholders.
- **Removed Explore Seed Sections from Home Page (`HomeScreen.tsx`):**
  - Removed the `Explore Kurnool Tourism` (`SEED_PLACES`) and `Places of Worship` (`SEED_WORSHIP_PLACES`) sections from `HomeScreen.tsx`.

## 2. Subscription Features & Hot Deals (`frontend/` & `website/`)

- **Unified Subscription Pricing (Option A) Across App & Website:**
  - Synchronized `website/src/services/directoryService.ts`, `website/src/app/register-business/page.tsx`, `website/src/app/register-professional/page.tsx`, `website/src/app/business/dashboard/page.tsx`, and `frontend/src/screens/AdminDashboardScreen.tsx`:
    - **Businesses:** Free (`₹0`) | Standard Verified (`₹399 / 6 Months`) | Premium Featured (`₹999 / 1 Year`)
    - **Professionals:** Free (`₹0`) | Standard Verified Pro (`₹199 / 6 Months`) | Elite Pro (`₹499 / 1 Year`)
- **4 Additional Gallery / Portfolio Images for Paid Subscribers:**
  - Added **Store Gallery Photos (up to 4)** in `ManageBusinessScreen.tsx` (`galleryUrls`).
  - Added **Work Portfolio Gallery (up to 4)** in `ManageProfessionalScreen.tsx` (`portfolioPhotos`).
  - Both are locked until `paymentStatus === 'paid'` and unlock automatically after subscription payment.
- **Calendar Date Picker & Auto-Expiry for Hot Deals:**
  - Created `frontend/src/components/CalendarDatePickerModal.tsx` and integrated it into `ManageBusinessScreen.tsx` and `ManageProfessionalScreen.tsx`, replacing manual text entry for offer validity.
  - Added `expiresAt` ISO date storage and automatic expiry filtering in `fetchOffers()` across both `frontend/src/services/directoryService.ts` and `website/src/services/directoryService.ts`.
- **Hot Deals Discovery (`OffersScreen.tsx`):**
  - Replaced the restrictive 5-category filter bar in Hot Deals with a real-time keyword Search Bar (`Search any shop, service, or discount...`) so all 50+ business and 30+ professional categories can post and be discovered freely.
